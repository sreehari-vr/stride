The brief

Get AGAM's UK tour in front of the right audience in every tour city, and turn attention into ticket-page visits, on a tight budget.

How it works

  1. 01

    Announce the whole tour

    An all-cities poster set out the six dates and venues, from London on 7 November to Leicester on 16 November.

  2. 02

    City-by-city creative

    Each city got its own poster, in blue, stage-colour and red looks, cut for square and 9:16 story placements so every ad ran native.

  3. 03

    Test, then commit

    Awareness and traffic ads ran side by side, and budget followed whichever performed best. The result was a £0.04 cost per link click and a 5.40% CTR.

  4. 04

    Urgency as tickets ran down

    Early-bird and countdown messaging pushed the ticket message in the run-up to each show.

  5. 05

    Paid and organic, together

    Ads were planned with the tour's own Facebook and Instagram pages, which grew 570% and 611.7% in views over the campaign.
